== Frequently Asked Questions

_Short questions. Shorter answers. Each answer points to the chapter with the
full story._

=== How do I register a new directive at runtime?

Call `register_directive(name, func)`, from ``navdict``'s directive module.
This adds a new `Setup` directive, such as `class//` or `csv//`, to the
running process immediately. No entry point and no restart is needed. See
Chapter 4, Section 3.4, "Special Value Directives."

=== How do I reset a memoized settings key?

You cannot reset one key. `Settings.clear_memoized()` clears the whole cache,
for every settings file. To force one file to reload, call `Settings.load(filename, force=True)`
instead. See Chapter 4, Section 3.3, "Memoization in `read_configuration_file`."
