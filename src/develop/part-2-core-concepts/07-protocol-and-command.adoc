== Protocols and Commanding

_How a method call becomes a command, and a command becomes a wire request._

Modules: `egse/protocol.py`, `egse/command.py` (both in `cgse-core`)

Chapter 6 introduced `ControlServer` and `Proxy`/`BaseProxy` as the client/server skeleton, and left `_request_commands()` and the abstract protocol/port contract as open threads. This chapter picks those up: what a `Command` actually is, and how a device-specific `CommandProtocol` subclass turns it into the dictionary `Proxy._request_commands()` consumes.
